How I Work With LLMs

I build the plumbing around models rather than the models themselves: agent backends, MCP tools, prompt and context pipelines, and the gates that decide whether an agent's output is allowed to land. Same principle as the product work — every step leaves inspectable evidence, and nothing ships on a model's word alone.
